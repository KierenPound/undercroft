Everything in one menu

Open the app and every part of the estate is one tap away - structured sections, each with its own checklist, notes, photos and documents, laid out in plain language.

What to Do First

The immediate steps in order - who to ring, where to register the death, and the details you will be asked for.

Legal Documents

Where the will, deeds and certificates are kept, and what your solicitors are holding for you.

Confirmation & Probate

The court process for your region, broken into ordered steps with the forms, fees and contact details.

Cars

Who to notify, insurance to sort, and what to do with the vehicle itself - such as insurance and paperwork.

Funeral Arrangements

Your own wishes and any funeral plan details, so nobody has to guess what you would have wanted.

Running the House

Your key tradespeople, where the meters are, and free format notes - the things someone needs to keep a home going.

Who to Notify

Every organisation and person to tell, with the contact details and what each one needs from you.

Pets & Ad Hoc Data

Vets, insurance, microchips - plus a separate section you define for anything particular to your household.

The parts that aren't admin

An estate is more than a list of tasks. UnderCroft keeps room for the things people are actually going to want, alongside the paperwork.

  • A letter to the people you love Write it in the app and it stays with the estate, ready to be read.
  • Your songs Build a playlist of songs you would want remembered, played or passed on.
  • Video Record something in your own voice for a moment that matters.
  • Memories Add a picture to most sections, so you can personalise the app.

Built to be trusted with family information

This is the most personal information you will ever write down. It is handled accordingly - and you can see that in how the app behaves.

  • Locked behind your face or finger Biometric access and a background timeout, so an unattended phone is not an open book.
  • Only you can read your records Data is stored in cloud databases protected by Row Level Security, with encryption in transit and at rest.
  • Never sold, never advertised against No selling, renting or trading of your information, and no advertising networks.
  • Delete it all whenever you choose Remove your account and every entry from Settings in a few taps.

How it is built

Built by me, Kieren Pound, as a small, independent product. No venture capital, no data-hungry platform. The architecture is deliberately simple, and I am happy to talk through any of it.

  • Built with AI as a core collaborator The product and architecture are mine, but much of the implementation was built with Trae as my primary coding partner - writing code, refactoring and diagnosing issues alongside me.
  • Code review and testing Code changes and test scenarios are reviewed and worked through with the help of tools such as Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT before anything ships.
  • Flutter, Supabase and Cloudflare R2 A native app on a secure backend, with files stored on Cloudflare's object storage.
  • Row Level Security Every record is locked to the estate it belongs to. The database itself enforces who can read what - not just the app.
  • Encrypted in transit and at rest Data is encrypted on the way in and out, and again where it is stored.
  • Passwords are not stored by design Most organisations require formal notification from an executor - handing over credentials would not help and would create a false sense of security. UnderCroft points you towards the right process instead.

Your plans stay private

Your estate stays private until you choose to invite a co-owner or executor. You decide when to share it, and access can be revoked.

Not ready to share digitally? Print an invite to keep with your papers or leave with your solicitor for later. Access is granted when the invite code is used.

Creating your own estate is free. Unlocking invites for that estate is a one-time payment, not a subscription.
