What's inside

Eight guided sections cover the practical side of an estate. Each one holds its own checklist, notes, photos and documents, and tells you where to go next rather than leaving you to work it out.

What to Do First

The immediate steps in order — who to ring, where to register the death, and the details you will be asked for.

Legal Documents

Where the will, deeds and certificates are kept, and what your solicitors are holding for you.

Confirmation & Probate

The court process for your region, broken into ordered steps with the forms, fees and contact details.

Cars

Who to notify, insurance to sort, and what to do with the vehicle itself — keys, paperwork and all.

Funeral Arrangements

Wishes, readings, music and the people to contact, so nobody has to guess what you would have wanted.

Running the House

Utilities, bills, accounts and access details — the things someone needs to keep a home going.

Who to Notify

Every organisation and person to tell, with the contact details and what each one needs from you.

Pets & Ad Hoc Details

Vets, insurance, microchips — plus a free-form section for anything particular to your household.

The parts that aren't admin

An estate is more than a list of tasks. UnderCroft keeps room for the things people are actually going to want, alongside the paperwork.

  • A letter to the people you love Write it in the app and it stays with the estate, ready to be read.
  • Your songs and a page's music Build a playlist, and choose the music for each part of the service.
  • A video message Record something in your own voice and hand it on for a moment that matters.
  • Photos of belongings and places Add pictures to any section, so there is no doubt about what you meant.

Built to be trusted with family information

This is the most personal information you will ever write down. It is handled accordingly — and you can see that in how the app behaves.

  • Locked behind your face or finger Biometric access and a background timeout, so an unattended phone is not an open book.
  • Only you can read your records Data is stored in cloud databases protected by Row Level Security, with encryption in transit and at rest.
  • Never sold, never advertised against No selling, renting or trading of your information, and no advertising networks.
  • Delete it all whenever you choose Remove your account and every entry from Settings in a few taps.

How it is built

Built by me, Kieren Pound, as a small, independent product. No venture capital, no data-hungry platform. The architecture is deliberately simple, and I am happy to talk through any of it.

  • Flutter, Supabase and Cloudflare R2 A native app on a secure backend, with files stored on Cloudflare's object storage.
  • Row Level Security Every record is locked to the estate it belongs to. The database itself enforces who can read what — not just the app.
  • Encrypted in transit and at rest Data is encrypted on the way in and out, and again where it is stored.
  • Account passwords are not stored by design Most organisations require formal notification from an executor — handing over credentials would not help and would create a false sense of security. UnderCroft points you towards the right process instead.
  • Built with AI-assisted development Code review and testing by Claude, automated app testing by ChatGPT, and content and UI review by Gemini — alongside human external beta testers. AI is a tool in the process, not a replacement for careful, deliberate work.

Shared with the right people

Preparing an estate is rarely a solo job. An owner can invite an executor or a co-owner to work alongside them, and take that access away again if circumstances change. Nobody else can see inside.
