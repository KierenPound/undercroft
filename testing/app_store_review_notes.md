# UnderCroft — App Store Review Notes

Working draft of the text that goes in **App Store Connect → App Review Information → Notes**
for build `1.0.0 (39)`.

Part 1 is paste-ready. Part 2 lists what only you can supply — submission is not possible
until those are filled in.

---

## 1. Paste-ready notes

```text
UnderCroft is an estate preparation app. Everything is private to one account; the app
requires an internet connection (Supabase backend).

SIGN-IN
UnderCroft requires an account. New sign-ups must confirm their email address before they
can sign in, so please use the demo account below rather than registering:

    Email:    <FILL IN: demo account email>
    Password: <FILL IN: demo account password>

This account owns a populated estate, so every screen has content to show without you
entering data.

APP LOCK (FACE ID)
The optional app lock (Settings > Privacy > "Unlock with biometrics") is OFF by default and
nothing in the app requires it. When switched on, the app covers itself with an unlock
screen after it has been in the background for more than 60 seconds, so estate records are
not visible in the app switcher. Face ID only hides the app's own content.

IN-APP PURCHASE
There is one non-consumable in-app purchase: "Complete the Handover"
(com.kierenpound.undercroft.handover). It unlocks invitations for one estate — bringing an
executor or co-owner in. Every other feature is free; nothing is gated except invitations.

To review the purchase, sign in as the demo account (it is the estate owner):
  1. Settings > Developer tools > switch "Test Payment Logic" on.
  2. Return to Settings — the "Invite Executor" card now shows as locked.
  3. Tap "Unlock" to open the store payment sheet. "Restore" is on the same card.

"Developer tools" is a build-time switch that lets the paid gate be rehearsed before the
purchase is enabled for all users. With it off, invitations are free, as they are in this
build by default.

PERMISSIONS
Photo library, camera and microphone are requested only at the moment you attach a picture
or record a video, and the prompt explains why. UnderCroft does not collect location; iOS
may show a location prompt when you pick a photo, which is system behaviour and not a
permission the app uses.

YOUTUBE CONTENT
"Song for the page" plays a public YouTube video through the official embed. It needs a
network connection and is the only third-party content in the app.

CONTENT AND SHARING
All content is private to the account that created it. Nothing is public, and there is no
feed, comments or user-to-user messaging. The owner can invite named people by email to
view an estate; invitations are generated by the owner, not posted publicly.

DELETING AN ACCOUNT
Settings > "Delete my account" removes the account and its estate in-app, as required by
guideline 5.1.1(v).

PRIVACY POLICY AND SUPPORT
https://crabkingslair.com/privacy
https://crabkingslair.com/support
A short demo guide is linked in-app under Settings > "Demo guide".
```

## 2. Must be filled in before submitting

| Item | Why it blocks review |
|---|---|
| Demo account email + password | Sign-up requires email confirmation ([auth_view.dart](file:///Users/kierenpound/Developer/UnderCroft/lib/views/auth_view.dart#L168-L173)), so a reviewer cannot create a working account themselves. Guideline 2.1 rejection risk. |
| A populated estate on that demo account | The notes say every screen has content. Needs to actually be true, or the claim should be dropped. |
| Whether `Test Payment Logic` ships enabled | The notes above assume it is **off** by default. If you ship it on, the invite card appears locked to a reviewer with no way past it. |

Two things I could not confirm from the code and did not assert:

- **Sign in with Apple.** Only email/password exists ([auth_view.dart](file:///Users/kierenpound/Developer/UnderCroft/lib/views/auth_view.dart#L178-L183)). If you also offer Google or Facebook sign-in anywhere, Sign in with Apple becomes mandatory (4.8) — add it to the notes or implement it.
- **The demo account's estate.** No seeded review estate exists in `supabase/`.

## 4. Likely rejection risks the notes are written to pre-empt

1. **2.1 — cannot sign in.** The demo account line is the fix. Do not rely on a reviewer registering.
2. **3.1.1 — IAP not obvious.** The step-by-step route to the purchase, including the product ID and that it is non-consumable, is stated explicitly.
3. **5.1.1(v) — account deletion.** Present in Settings; called out so the reviewer does not have to hunt for it.
4. **2.3.1 — hidden features.** The "Developer tools" section is disclosed and explained rather than left for the reviewer to find and wonder about.
5. **4.8 — Sign in with Apple.** Only an issue if other third-party sign-in is added. Flagged in Part 2.

## 5. Not evidenced in the code, so deliberately omitted

Stated here so the notes are not read as claiming more than the build does:

- No push notifications are implemented, so none are mentioned.
- The purchase unlock is UI-only for the session. Durable per-estate entitlements, server-side
  store verification and refund revocation are recorded as outstanding in
  [MONETISATION.md](file:///Users/kierenpound/Developer/UnderCroft/MONETISATION.md#L52-L74).
  The reviewer-facing notes describe what the build does today and say the gate is rehearsable,
  which is accurate. Be aware that a reviewer buying it will not get durable access.
